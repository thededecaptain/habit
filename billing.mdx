Habit has one published plan. There is no order-count cliff and no features gated behind a higher tier.

## Standard plan

- **$49 USD / month** (billed every 30 days)
- **30-day trial** — you are not charged until the trial ends
- Points, VIP tiers, referrals, refund clawback, and storefront widgets are included

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Billing">
    In Habit, go to **Billing**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Start the trial">
    Click **Start 30-day free trial**. Shopify confirms the charge. Until this is done, admin screens redirect back here.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Cancel

Cancel from **Settings**, not from the Billing page.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Settings">
    In Habit, go to **Settings**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Cancel subscription">
    Click **Cancel subscription**, then confirm. Billing stops immediately. Unused time in the current period is credited.
  </Step>
</Steps>

You can start a new trial later from **Billing**. Uninstalling Habit from Shopify also ends the subscription. You do not need to email support to leave.

## Questions about a charge

Email [support@gethabitloyalty.com](mailto:support@gethabitloyalty.com) with your shop domain and the charge date. Include a screenshot from **Settings → Billing** in Shopify if you have one.
