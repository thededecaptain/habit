Habit has one published plan. There is no order-count cliff and no features gated behind a higher tier.

## Standard plan

- **$29 USD / month**
- **14-day trial**
- Points, VIP tiers, referrals, checkout redemption, and refund clawback are all included

Start the trial from **Billing** after you install, or when Shopify prompts you.

## Cancel

You can cancel yourself:

1. Open Habit in Shopify admin.
2. Go to **Billing**.
3. Click **Cancel**.

That stops the recurring charge. You do not need to email support or wait for a reply. Uninstalling the app from Shopify also ends the subscription.

## Questions about a charge

Email [support@gethabitloyalty.com](mailto:support@gethabitloyalty.com) with your shop domain and the charge date. Include a screenshot from **Settings → Billing** in Shopify if you have one.
