# Marketing site checklist (gethabitloyalty.com)

The marketing site is **not** in this repo. Update it manually before App Store submission so merchants and reviewers see consistent pricing.

## Must match the live app

- [ ] **Price:** $49 USD / month (billed every 30 days) — not $29
- [ ] **Trial:** 30-day free trial — not 14 days
- [ ] **Billing:** Charged through Shopify App Pricing; cancel in app Settings
- [ ] **Notifications:** Shopify Flow triggers + optional webhook — no “paste your Klaviyo API key in Habit”
- [ ] **Support email:** support@gethabitloyalty.com
- [ ] **Privacy / terms links:** https://gethabitloyalty.com/privacy and /terms

## Recommended copy snippets

**Hero subhead:** Points, VIP tiers, and referrals on one flat plan — $49/month after a 30-day trial.

**Notifications section:** Habit sends loyalty events to Shopify Flow. Connect Klaviyo, Shopify Email, or Slack through Flow, or POST JSON to your own webhook.

## Verify after editing

1. Open gethabitloyalty.com in a private window.
2. Confirm pricing appears on homepage and any pricing page.
3. Click **Install** / App Store link → lands on correct Partner app (`habit-loyalty`).
