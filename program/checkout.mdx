---
title: Checkout redemption
description: Redeem points in the cart on every plan, or at checkout on Shopify Plus.
---

Customers redeem points as a checkout discount. You do not need Shopify Scripts. A Shopify Function applies the discount.

## Where customers redeem

| Surface | Who gets it |
|---|---|
| [Redeem points in cart](/storefront#redeem-in-the-cart) app embed or cart-page block | All Shopify plans |
| Checkout UI extension | Shopify Plus only |

The customer must be signed in. Guest checkouts cannot spend points or attach a referral code to a customer account.

## Customer flow

<Steps>
  <Step title="Meet the minimum">
    The balance must be at least **Minimum points to redeem**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Apply points">
    In cart (every plan) or checkout (Plus), the customer chooses how many points to spend. Habit caps that against **Max % of order payable with points** on the **subtotal**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pay">
    On a successful paid order, those points leave the ledger. If both cart attributes and checkout metafields are set, the checkout value wins.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Referral codes use the same two surfaces. See [referrals](/program/referrals).

## If redemption is missing

<Steps>
  <Step title="Confirm billing">
    A trial or Standard plan must be active so the app stays installed and the Function discount can run.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm the cart embed">
    On non-Plus stores, enable **Redeem points in cart** in theme **App embeds**. The checkout points field will not appear.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Test as a logged-in customer">
    Place a test order with a customer account that has a points balance.
  </Step>
</Steps>

If it still does not appear, email [support@gethabitloyalty.com](mailto:support@gethabitloyalty.com) with the shop domain.

## After a refund

Refunding an order claws back a proportional share of points earned on that order. See [points](/program/points#refunds) if those points were already spent.
