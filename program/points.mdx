---
title: Points
description: How customers earn points on order subtotal, redeem them, and what refunds and expiry do to the balance.
---

Every Habit program shares one points ledger. Purchases, referrals, VIP multipliers, and manual adjustments all write to the same balance.

## Earn on purchase

When an order is paid, Habit credits points using **Points earned per $1 spent**, applied to the **order subtotal**. Tax and shipping do not earn points. VIP multipliers apply on top of that rate.

Example: if the rate is 1 point per $1 and the subtotal is $48, the customer earns 48 points at the 1x base rate.

The multiplier used is the customer’s **current** tier. If that order pushes them into a higher tier, the new multiplier starts on the next paid order.

Points only post after the order is paid. Drafts, unpaid checkouts, and guest orders with no customer do not earn.

## Redeem

Customers spend points as a discount. Conversion uses **Points needed for $1 off**, plus your minimum balance and max percent of the **subtotal**.

They can redeem:

- In the **cart**, on every Shopify plan
- At **checkout**, on Shopify Plus

See [storefront and checkout](/storefront) and [checkout redemption](/program/checkout).

## Refunds

Refunding an order claws back points in proportion to the refunded amount versus the original subtotal. A full refund reverses the full earn for that order. A 50% refund reverses about half.

You do not need to adjust the ledger by hand.

If those points were already spent, the balance can go negative until future purchases cover it. Habit does not delete later redemptions.

## Points expiry

Optionally set **Expire unused points after (days)** in Settings. Unused points expire after that many days with no earn-on-order or redemption. The whole balance expires together — this is not oldest-points-first.

Leave the field empty to keep points indefinitely. Referral bonuses and [manual adjustments](#manual-adjustments) do not reset the expiry clock.

## Manual adjustments

Open **Customers** in Habit, pick a member, and add or subtract points. A reason is required. Use a negative number to subtract.

Manual adjustments appear in the member’s history as an adjustment. They change the balance immediately but do not count as purchase activity for expiry.
