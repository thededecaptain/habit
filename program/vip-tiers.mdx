---
title: VIP tiers
description: Reward repeat customers with spend or order thresholds and higher earn rates. Tiers never drop.
---

VIP tiers give a higher earn multiplier once a customer hits a lifetime spend or order count you choose. You can add as many tiers as you need. Members earn the base Settings rate until you create at least one tier.

## How tiers work

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Tiers">
    In Habit, go to **Tiers**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add a tier">
    Set a name, a minimum lifetime spend and/or minimum lifetime orders, an earn multiplier, and a sort order. You must set spend, orders, or both.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save">
    Habit assigns the qualifying tier with the **highest sort order**. Give better tiers a higher sort number so they win when a customer qualifies for more than one.
  </Step>
</Steps>

A 1.5x multiplier on a 1-point-per-dollar program means a $40 **subtotal** earns 60 points.

Tiers are based on lifetime spend and orders and **never drop**. Deleting a tier unassigns it; customers can qualify again on a later order.

The order that crosses a threshold still earns at the previous multiplier. The new rate starts on the next paid order.

## What customers see

Signed-in customers see their current tier (and progress to the next) on the [loyalty widget](/storefront) and the customer account rewards block, when those are enabled.
